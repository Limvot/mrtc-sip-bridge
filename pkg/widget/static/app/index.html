Set up this app

Send dialpad-link to your bridge bot in your management room (your direct chat with the bot) to get a link. Then tap Paste link below and paste the message, or open the link on this phone.

On iPhone, add this app to the Home Screen first, open it from there, then paste. A link used in Safari does not carry over to the Home Screen app.

Set up this device?

Calls from numbers not in your contacts go to voicemail.

This signs this phone out of the bridge. To use it again you need a new link.
